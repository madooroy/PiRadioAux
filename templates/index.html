Shutting down

Wait until the Pi's green light stops flickering and stays off (about 30 seconds), then unplug it.

To start it again, plug it back in.

Favorites

Songs you saved with the heart. “Find in Spotify” opens a Spotify search for the song, where you can play it or add it to your Liked Songs.

Edit presets

Each preset needs a name and the station's direct stream address (starts with http:// or https://). The now-playing feed is optional and only needed when a station doesn't show the artist and song by itself.

Export
Searches the community Radio Browser directory. Tap a result to add it as a preset.
